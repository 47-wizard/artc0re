"Who am I.. really?"

originally written: 2026-10-10 | last updated: 2026-10-29

Greeting!

You might have found your way here via my bio on most of my SNS, or perhaps I gave you the actual site address in real life. Either way, I wish you stay healthy and find actual peace in this “chaotic, messiness” life.

⋅ Why did this site exist?

TL;DR: I just love writing things out.

Having gone through lots of terrible experiences in the past, writing and music have been the only ways I’ve found to express my feelings for years. I’ve been fighting the idea of staying silent or doing the opposite.. pumping out an unnecessary amount of words. I’m a person who would say really.. unnecessary really much. And I hurted people around me, either by writing unnecessary letter about my past.. that took me nights to write them down, either to feel shame afterwards, or by acting cold to make it look like I was fine, but tbh.. I wasn’t. Words are inherently a powerful tool that can ruin someone’s day, just by saying the wrong thing outside, and I did that in the face of terrible mental illness. But… I still kept writing. To either acknowledge my mistakes, express how I felt about the things I had been through or just encourage myself to do better. And I hope my blogs will inspire you, or at least… make you feel something inside yours.

⋅ So, who are you?

Kenji-san (sometimes styled as K3nji, or Kasanji). Tho, people who already knew me in the past would recognize me as Olly (as it was my really old name used throughout my childhood till high school). I would love to be called they/ them, since having a fixed gender does not fit me at all, which indeed, I’m fluid. Sometimes, I don’t want to assign myself to any gender either way. I rarely have any interested in love currently.. due to my past trauma i guess?

I live on the Internet since the age of nine, and it has been a huge part of my identity. In real life, I’m not good at talking “casually” or reacting to someone’s jokes. I’m really slow to understand most of the jokes that people around me try to cheer me up and I would take a significant amounts of time/context to fully understand them. What I mean is that I am boring lol, since I always prefer “nature” instead of “humanity” noises. I hate noisy environment (e.g. super loud events) and have a tendency to run away from them, which does explain why I just love nature so so much. And weirdly, in this modern age of “ATTENTION THEFT”, I’m intented with the use of SNS, since it always draw my attention to million pathways. I barely scroll, in fact… I don’t have most mainstream SNSs either (e.g. Instagram, TikTok, etc.). I keep the use of YouTube and Twitter and keep the feed clean. Of course, in objectively to join so called ‘modern society’, I have to some SNS/IM (instant messenger) that I don’t want to use, but I keep it solely for communication purpose.

That might sounds a little bit negative, but it just that I prefer to be alone, or at much I can say.. truly be able to enjoy and question more, with myself. I can communicate for work-related stuff and try to keep it that way, as long as it won’t affect my energy (which usually boils down to questions about my personal life). And.. because I’m so damn sensitive, I’m being too easy to smile.. at everywhere (and I hate it lol).

also… my taste in music is a total disaster lol.

Appreciate for reading this far! More stuff to come along the way..
