### ⋅ Why did this site exist?

*TL;DR: I just love writing things out.*

Having gone through lots of terrible experiences in the past, writing and music have been the only ways I've found to express my feelings for years. I've been fighting the idea of staying silent or doing the opposite.. pumping out an unnecessary amount of words. I'm a person who would say really.. unnecessary really much. And I hurted people around me, either by writing unnecessary letter about my past.. that took me nights to write them down, either to feel shame afterwards, or by acting cold to make it look like I was fine, but tbh.. I wasn't. Words are inherently a powerful tool that can ruin someone's day, just by saying the wrong thing outside, and I did that in the face of ~~terrible mental illness~~. But... I still kept writing. To either acknowledge my mistakes, express how I felt about the things I had been through or just encourage myself to do better. And I hope my blogs will inspire you, or at least... make you feel something inside yours.
